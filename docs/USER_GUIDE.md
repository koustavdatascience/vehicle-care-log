# Vehicle Care Log User Guide

Vehicle Care Log helps you keep fuel, service, repair, reminder, and expense records for each vehicle. Your day-to-day records are stored **on your device**, so the core app works without signing in or being connected to the internet.

## Install the Android APK

1. Download `vehicle-care-log-v1.0.2.apk` from the repository’s **Releases** page.
2. Open the downloaded file on your Android phone.
3. If Android asks for permission, allow the browser or file manager you used to install this one app. Install only APKs obtained from the official Vehicle Care Log release page.
4. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.

> If Android says that installation is blocked, open the relevant app’s settings, allow installs from that source, complete the installation, and turn the permission off again if you do not normally install APKs.

## Start with a vehicle

Open **My garage** and tap **Add vehicle**. Enter the vehicle’s nickname, make, model, year, and current odometer reading. The nickname is what you will see when selecting a vehicle throughout the app.

| Task | Where to do it |
|---|---|
| Switch between vehicles | Tap the vehicle selector on the Home screen. |
| Edit a vehicle | Open **My garage**, then tap the vehicle card. |
| Add another vehicle | Open **My garage** and tap **Add vehicle**. |
| Choose the active vehicle | Tap a vehicle in **My garage**; the active label moves to that vehicle. |

## Add care records

Use the **plus** action from Home to choose the kind of record you want to add. Enter the date, odometer value, amount, and any fields that apply to the selected record type.

| Record type | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Fuel | Fuel fill-ups, litres, price, and odometer readings. |
| Service | Scheduled maintenance such as oil, filters, tyres, or inspection work. |
| Repair | Unexpected fixes, replacement parts, and repair costs. |

The Home and History views use these saved records to show recent activity, expenses, and useful summaries for the active vehicle.

## Create reminders

Open **Reminders** and add a reminder for a date or an odometer interval. To receive alerts, open **My garage**, turn on **Local reminder notifications**, and allow notifications when Android asks. Reminders are scheduled on the device; internet access is not required for local notifications.

## Export a CSV report

Open **My garage** and find **Local CSV report**. Choose a vehicle and a date range, then tap **Export CSV report**. The app creates a spreadsheet-ready file containing that vehicle’s fuel, service, and repair entries, and opens the Android share sheet.

Choose **Custom** in the date-range selector when you need an exact start date, end date, or an open-ended range. The dates are inclusive: a record on the selected start or end date is included. The export does not include private details such as registration labels, free-form notes, account details, sync metadata, or diagnostics.

## Keep your records safe

Your records are designed to stay on your device. Keep the phone backed up through the device method you trust, and export a CSV copy before resetting or replacing your phone. A CSV export is useful for sharing a service history, but it is not a complete restore backup for the app.

## Need help?

When seeking help, open **My garage** and note the app version shown in Settings. Include the phone model, Android version, and the steps that led to the problem. Do not include vehicle registration numbers or other private data in a public issue.
